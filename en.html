CompraGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how CompraGuard handles information when you use the Android application. For privacy enquiries, contact labschann@gmail.com.

1. Your library stays on your device

CompraGuard helps organise purchases, amounts, dates, merchants, notes, receipts, warranties, returns, claims and reminders. Information you enter and documents you select are stored in the application's private storage on your device. CompraGuard does not provide family synchronisation or automatically upload this library to its own server.

You do not need a CompraGuard account. The application does not integrate Firebase, Supabase or a separate analytics or crash reporting service. Google services used for advertising and purchases do process data, as explained below.

2. Google AdMob advertising

The free edition may show Google AdMob banners. The advertising SDK may collect and share with Google your IP address, which can estimate general location; device identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID; identifiers related to device accounts where applicable; interactions with the application and advertisements; and diagnostic and performance information. Google uses these data for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention.

CompraGuard does not send the contents of your purchases, notes or receipts as advertising targeting parameters. It does not request precise location for advertising. Data transmitted by the AdMob SDK are encrypted in transit using TLS.

The application uses Google User Messaging Platform to request and manage privacy choices where applicable. Advertising requests depend on this platform allowing them. When Google requires a privacy options entry point, you can review your choices through “Ad privacy options” in “Data and backups”. You can also manage your advertising ID and ad preferences through Android settings and your Google account.

3. One-time Premium purchase and Google Play

Google Play handles the one-time Premium purchase, which removes advertisements after the purchase is confirmed. CompraGuard queries Google Play for the purchased product and its status and uses the purchase identifier or token to acknowledge payment. It does not receive card numbers or payment credentials. Purchase tokens are not included in library exports.

Restoring Premium depends on the Google Play account used to purchase it. This product has no family subscription or recurring renewal. Removing advertisements does not erase data Google has already processed or stop processing necessary to manage your purchase.

4. Permissions, language and preferences

Internet access is used for Google Play, advertising and managing privacy choices. Notification permission, if granted, allows local reminders. The document picker lets you decide which files to import and where to save exports; the application does not need general access to all your files.

Initially, CompraGuard follows Android's default language among supported languages. You can select a different language or return to “Device language” in “Data and backups”. Android and AndroidX store the language preference. These preferences are not sent to an application server.

5. Exports and shared files

PDF reports and ZIP exports contain the data and documents you choose to include and are not encrypted by CompraGuard. A password-protected CGB backup provides optional library encryption. You choose the destination application or location when exporting or sharing. If you choose email, cloud storage or another application, that recipient's practices also apply. External copies are not deleted when you delete your local library.

6. Retention, security and deletion

Your library remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete library data in “Data and backups”. To delete preferences and other local application data as well, clear the application's data in Android settings or uninstall it. Exports stored outside the application must be deleted separately.

The application uses private storage and Android protections. CompraGuard does not fully encrypt the local database; protection also depends on your device security. No system can guarantee absolute security. Google retains data according to its own policies; deleting CompraGuard does not automatically delete those data.

7. Providers, transfers and your rights

Google provides advertising and payment services and may process information in different countries under its policies and applicable mechanisms. See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from applications that use its services and AdMob SDK data disclosures.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object, restrict processing, portability and withdrawal of consent, and to complain to a competent authority. Contact the privacy email above about CompraGuard processing. Your local library is controlled on your device; for data processed directly by Google, also use Google's privacy controls and channels. If you send us an enquiry, we use the data you provide to respond and retain them only as necessary to resolve it and meet applicable obligations. Avoid sending receipts or sensitive information unless necessary.

8. This page and policy updates

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages without forms, advertisements, our own tracking tools or external design resources. GitHub may process technical visit information, such as IP addresses, under its Privacy Statement. The application does not send your library when opening this link.

We will update this page when the described practices change and indicate the update date. Publishing this policy does not replace your consent choices.
